3. Card style

Borderless Overlay is the current project style. Full Art keeps full-card artwork with translucent information panels. Standard Frame uses an art window and separate rules area. Freeform lets you select, drag, lock, hide, and layer every major card element.

Upload transparent PNG/WebP overlays for individual frame pieces. These are available in Freeform, Full Art, and Standard. Use transparent graphics so artwork/text can show through.
